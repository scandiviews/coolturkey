Why this desk exists

There is an enormous amount of hip hop media covering who is beefing with whom, and almost none covering the fact that your distributor quietly takes 20% of your TikTok money. The creative side of independent rap is over-served, while the administrative side, the part that decides whether you keep your money, is barely covered at all.

Rap Ledger is the second one. Every number here is dated and sourced. When something changes, the page changes and says so. Opinions live over in Cold Takes, because this desk stays boring on purpose.
